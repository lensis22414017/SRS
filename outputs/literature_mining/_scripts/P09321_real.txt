A B S T R A C T
Polycyclic aromatic hydrocarbons (PAHs) are persistent organic pollutants that pose significant environmental 
and health risks, especially in rapidly urbanizing cities such as Hangzhou, China. Understanding the spatio­
temporal dynamics of PAH emissions, distribution, and health risks is essential for developing effective control 
strategies. In this study, PAH emissions in Hangzhou from 2010 to 2022 were estimated and their multimedia 
environmental distribution was simulated using a Level IV fugacity model. Python (with PyCharm 2022.3.3) was 
used to perform modeling, data processing, and visualization. Annual PAH emissions (t/year) increased from 
158.33 in 2010 to a peak of 205.53 in 2018, then declined to 193.96 by 2022. Simulated concentrations showed 
that soil and sediment act as long-term sinks for PAHs, while aerosol-phase concentrations in the atmosphere 
respond most rapidly to changes in emissions. Future projections suggest a compositional shift from high mo­
lecular weight (HMW) to low molecular weight (LMW) PAHs. An Incremental Lifetime Cancer Risk (ILCR) model 
was applied to evaluate human exposure risks, showing that sediment is the dominant exposure medium, 
particularly via dermal contact and inhalation. This study provides a dynamic, long-term assessment of PAH

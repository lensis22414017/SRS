Occurrence and health risk assessment of phthalate esters in tobacco and
soils in tobacco-producing areas of Guizhou province, southwest China

HIGHLIGHTS

· Phthalate esters (PAEs) in fresh tobacco leaves and soils were studied in southwest China.
· DEHP was the most abundant of the total PAEs both in soils and tobacco.
· Plastic films were a major source of PAEs in tobacco cultivation.
· The carcinogenic and non-cancer risks of PAEs were within an acceptable level.

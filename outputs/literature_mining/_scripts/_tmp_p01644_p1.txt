A B S T R A C T
Artisanal and small-scale mining and processing (ASMP) remain pervasive in developing countries, causing se­
vere heavy metal(loids) (HMs) pollution and complicating source apportionment. Current understanding remains 
limited regarding the distribution of metallurgical organic pollutants (MOPs) and their potential role in HM 
source apportionment. In this study, representative MOPs, xanthates and polycyclic aromatic hydrocarbons 
(PAHs), were selected and analyzed in an abandoned non-ferrous tailings pond within Guizhou’s mercury mining 
areas, China. It presents the first quantitation of three flotation xanthates (ethyl-, isopropyl-, and butyl-xanthate)
